About Premier Industries

Built on precision.
Grown on trust.

Founded in 1995 as a small-scale proprietorship, Premier Industries has evolved into a premier surface treatment company serving India's largest automotive OEMs. From our facility in Thirumazhisai, Chennai, we deliver zinc plating, zinc-nickel plating and zinc-iron plating for demanding industrial and automotive parts.

In October 2020, we upgraded to a fully automated electroplating line supported by Grauer & Weil (India) Ltd., achieving zero breakdown performance with end-to-end PLC & SCADA control.

Our focus is disciplined process control: the right chemistry, repeatable deposits and a coating record that gives production and quality teams confidence.

1995

Established as a proprietorship unit with electroplating lines at Thirumazhisai SIDCO Industrial Estate.

1998

Became sole supplier to HL Mando Automotive India Pvt. Ltd. for brake caliper and carrier housing — a relationship that continues to this day.

2020

Upgraded to a fully automated electroplating line supported by Grauer & Weil (India) Ltd., achieving zero breakdown performance.
