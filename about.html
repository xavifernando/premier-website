Our story

Three decades of surface excellence.

Founded in 1995 as a small-scale unit at SIDCO Industrial Estate, Thirumazhisai, Premier Industries has evolved into a premier surface treatment company serving India’s largest automotive OEMs.

We specialise in zinc plating, zinc-nickel plating and zinc-iron plating for demanding industrial and automotive parts — especially brake calipers and carriers.

In October 2020 we upgraded to a fully automated electroplating line supported by Grauer & Weil (India) Ltd., achieving zero-breakdown performance with end-to-end PLC & SCADA control.
