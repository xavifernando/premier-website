Services

Electroplating processes

Zinc alloy systems engineered for automotive corrosion protection, with automated control and documented quality.

01

Zinc Plating

Sacrificial zinc deposits protect steel from corrosion. Barrel and rack lines for high-volume fasteners and stampings.

  • Alkaline zinc vat & barrel
  • Acid zinc barrel
  • Clear, blue, black, yellow passivation (trivalent, Cr6-free)

02

Zinc-Nickel Plating

High-performance alloy for critical parts — superior salt-spray resistance and thermal stability vs pure zinc.

  • Automotive-ready protection
  • Ideal for brake system components
  • Complex geometry coverage

03

Zinc-Iron Plating

Engineered alloy balancing adhesion, appearance and corrosion performance for structural and finish-critical parts.

  • Uniform alloy deposits
  • Specification-led process control
  • Versatile finish choices

Hydrogen Embrittlement Relief

Box-type oven · 250°C · up to 1000 kg/load — essential post-plating for high-strength fasteners and safety parts.

Clear · Blue · Black · Yellow — all trivalent, Cr6-free systems aligned to automotive environmental requirements.
